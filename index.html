Elite reference v1

Pack manifest — 422,059 accepted archive records, 249,245 full scores, 684,269 aggregate positions. Explorer positions through ply 35; this is not 35 full moves.

Derived from the Lichess broadcast archive, retrieved 1 September 2026. Attribution: Lichess broadcast archive — lichess.org. Data license: CC BY-SA 4.0.

Transformation: rating/title/length filtering, position replay and aggregation, player indexes, sharding and gzip compression. The manifest records upstream and derived SHA-256 checksums.

This is the existing v1 dataset. It is a filtered broadcast collection, not proof of all official over-the-board games. Broadcast metadata and statistical deduplication are under audit; do not infer complete population coverage from the pack name. Deep-reference updates will have separate versioned paths.

Install only a manifest you trust. Checksums detect byte changes; they do not establish publisher authenticity or source accuracy.
